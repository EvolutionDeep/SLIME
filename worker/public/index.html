Inspired by the Anthropic Frontier Red Team post “AI models may be capable of self-replicating without access to a datacenter” (2026-10-12). This colony replays the post's dynamics as pure sandbox math:

  • capability floor — a child can only spawn if the inherited genome covers the challenge difficulty;
  • size ceiling — the genome must fit the host fleet (15 TV boxes at 0.39 tok/s aggregate);
  • Lamarckian notes — every agent inherits the parent's rewritten notebook; familiarity with seen hardware raises first-attempt odds (the post's 45% → 61–84% curve);
  • R₀ under caps — three-layer credit budgets keep the effective reproduction rate below 1: the strain goes endemic, never epidemic;
  • quarantine — a blue-team sweep cages random hosts once the colony grows; caged agents burn energy and starve out.

Simulation only — no real network activity, no model weights, no exploits. The Pit replays the same math: you train what the paper measured.
